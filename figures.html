The reactor, figure by figure

These six diagrams are the complete visual documentation for the OlivAir reactor platform — from the full four-zone cutaway down to the rod anode and gas-capture sleeve. Read alongside the OlivAir overview for the mechanisms each one supports.

Figure 1

Reactor cutaway — the four functional zones

Four functional zones stacked in a single vessel, acid and substrate entering at the base, CO₂ delivered through the hollow impeller shaft, wall-mounted hollow baffles doing double duty as reaction, collection, and gas-handling surfaces.

Platform architectureZone 0–3
Figure 2

Three hardware variants, one outer vessel

The three hardware variants the ten waste-stream cases collapse into — passive, partial-electrochemical, and fully electrochemical — differing only in which baffle walls carry an active cathode. Same outer vessel and Zone 0 geometry in all three.

Platform architecture10 waste streams → 3 hardware configs
Figure 3

Multi-function baffle wall — plan and cross-section

Detailed views of the modular electrochemical baffle wall used throughout the OlivAir platform. Plan view illustrates the offset-wall, louvered-front-face geometry, while the Zone 1 cross-section illustrates the protected-electrode isolated chamber. See Figure 4 for the rod anode and its gas-capture sleeve detail, shown separately.

Patent filedZone 1
Figure 4

Rod anode assembly and gas-capture sleeve

Isometric detail of the rod anode assembly and gas-capture sleeve. Each rod anode is wrapped along its full length by a perforated capture sleeve, sized to the anticipated gas bubble diameter, feeding a sealed riser to a single dedicated outlet at the unit's crown — physically isolated from the cathode's own H₂ outlet and mirroring the louver-based separation principle shown in Figure 3. Anode material is dimensionally-stable mixed-metal-oxide-coated titanium (Ru/Ir-oxide-coated for chlorine-evolving service, Ir-oxide-coated for oxygen-evolving service).

Zone 0 acid delivery geometry

Two classification mechanisms, in series

Zone 0's hydrodynamic classification by residence time, contrasted with Zones 1–3's precipitate-type and pH-window staging — two different classification mechanisms operating in series, not one mechanism spanning all four zones.
