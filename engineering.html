Patentable mechanisms

The three original mechanisms in the reactor design, all covered by the filed U.S. provisional patent application (No. 64/124,248).

01

Protected-electrode isolated chamber

An isolated-electrolyte chamber that shields active electrode surfaces from fouling within the reactor body, allowing electrochemical zones to run in fine-particle or corrosive streams that would otherwise damage exposed electrodes.

02

Integrated multi-function baffle wall

A single hollow structure that combines flow control, reaction staging, and (where required) electrode housing — reducing the reactor's part count versus separate baffles, staging tanks, and electrode fixtures.

03

Zone 0 hydrodynamic classification chamber

Sorts particles by settling behavior at reactor entry, before mineral dissolution begins — a dual-mode classification approach integrated directly into the entry zone rather than as a separate upstream unit.
