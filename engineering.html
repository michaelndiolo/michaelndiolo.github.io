Patentable mechanisms

Two mechanisms in the reactor are described in the filed U.S. provisional patent application (No. 64/124,248).

01

Zone 0 hydrodynamic classification chamber

Acid enters from a floor manifold and from nozzles in a confinement wall, adding into one net upward velocity. Coarse, unreacted grains stay low and finer, partly dissolved grains rise to an impeller, so each grain gets the time it needs in the acid.

02

Hollow baffle wall

A hollow unit on the vessel wall with two walls of different length and a slanted, louvered front face. The liquid circling the vessel meets the shorter wall first. The unit is meant to hold liquid longer and catch fines and precipitating metals.

How the design is checked

Cost model

A workbook of 10 sheets and 131 scenarios, set up per tonne of CO₂ and applying no credit. Every input is cited or flagged as an assumption, and a verification log records what was checked. See the results.

Chemistry

Dissolution and carbonate chemistry from the published work, including a calculated pH of about 3.6 for CO₂ at 5 bar in pure water. These are calculations, not measurements.

Bench tests

None has been run. The tests that matter most are the dissolution rate of real slag in carbonic acid and whether partly dissolved grains become lighter and rise. See the full list.
