How the OlivAir design is being checked: a lean cost model, 3D models, and a test plan that puts bench data and a vendor quote ahead of more modeling.

What is being designed

Two plants, both on paper. The mine drainage reactor is the part that is specific to OlivAir.

01

Fines plant (the fines lung)

Screen fresh slag fines, send the finest under 1 mm to a mine, and carbonate the 1 to 4 mm fraction in a covered bay with conditioned stack gas, two bays taking turns, until the free lime is used up.

02

Mine drainage reactor

Two fluidized slag beds in series, pulsed to keep the grains clean, with the slag moving against the water, aeration between the stages, and a clarifier to catch the iron floc.

How the design is checked

Cost model

A lean workbook covering the three ideas: capital, energy, labor, hauling and disposal. Every input is cited or flagged as a placeholder, and the equipment prices stay placeholders until a real vendor quote replaces them.

Published data

Figures taken from published lab work are re-derived from their stated conditions, for example the carbon uptake of tailings under a known gas flow, to see whether the numbers hold together before they go in the model.

Planned experiments (none has been run yet)

These follow the work Dr. McCormick sets. F5: free lime and swelling of carbonated fines in a covered bed. A leach test of the spent slag. A bench pulsed-bed test on real mine water. A processor's numbers for what fines cost them today, and a vendor quote to replace the placeholder equipment prices. Back to the overview.

Skills demonstrated

Reactor & process design

A two-stage pulsed fluidized-bed reactor and a covered-bay gas train, designed on paper from the chemistry up to equipment layout.

Techno-economic modeling

A lean workbook with capital recovery, energy, labor and disposal, built so that a changed price flows through every case.

Reviewing my own work

The design went through many review rounds and was cut from a long list of ideas to three, with the dropped ones written down and the reasons kept.

Technical writing

A living design document, a test plan, and 3D models that explain the plants to people who are not engineers.

What's next

Bench-scale validation.

The design is modeled at TRL 2. My own experiments come after the work Dr. McCormick sets, starting with F5, then real mine-water chemistry, alongside a vendor quote.
