Project 01 · at the steel mill

The fines lung

Fresh steel-slag fines swell when they get wet, so the fine sizes cannot go into roads or concrete. This design ages them in days, not months, and stores CO₂ while it does. Modeled on paper: nothing is built and nothing is measured.

The catch

It only pays if a processor really pays to age or landfill these fines, and I have not confirmed that. It stores about 4 t of CO₂ a day, so it is a fines process, not a carbon one. Test F5 decides.
