Independent research · TRL 2

OlivAir

A reactor that uses CO₂ dissolved in water as the acid to dissolve calcium-rich waste such as steel slag, with a vessel that controls how long each grain stays in it. Modeled so far, not yet built. Parts are covered by a filed U.S. provisional patent application.
