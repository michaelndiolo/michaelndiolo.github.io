OlivAir

A reactor platform that uses industrial acid waste as the thermodynamic driver for mineral dissolution and CO₂ capture — producing permanent solid carbonate instead of a neutralized sludge. Three mechanisms covered by a filed U.S. provisional patent application, documented across a ten-part paper series.
